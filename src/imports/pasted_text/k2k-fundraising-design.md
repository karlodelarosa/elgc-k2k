# K2K Church Fundraising Website — Figma Design Prompt

Design a modern, inspiring, one-page fundraising website for **K2K**, a church fundraising project of **Emmanuel's Living Gospel Church (ELGC)**.

## Project Context

K2K originally stood for:

**1,000 people × ₱1,000 = ₱1,000,000**

The campaign originally started around 15 years ago with the dream of raising ₱1 million to acquire a larger church property.

Today, the vision has grown. The original ₱1M target is no longer enough for the kind of property and ministry space we are praying for.

### New Goal

**₱10,000,000**

The purpose of K2K is to help ELGC acquire a larger and more suitable place of worship where the church can continue to grow, disciple people, worship God, serve the community, and reach more people with the Gospel.

This website should communicate:

* Faith
* Unity
* Generosity
* Vision
* Hope
* Progress
* Community
* "We can do this together."

The website should NOT feel like a corporate fundraising campaign or a generic donation website.

It should feel like a **movement of people building something together for God's Kingdom.**

---

# Overall Design Direction

Use the uploaded aesthetic/design reference as the primary visual inspiration.

Keep the design:

* Modern
* Premium
* Minimal
* Bold
* Emotional
* Faith-centered without feeling overly traditional
* Strong typography
* Excellent use of whitespace
* High visual impact
* Mobile-first and responsive
* Contemporary enough for young people while still feeling appropriate for the entire congregation

Use a visual language that feels like a **modern church campaign / movement website**, rather than a typical church homepage.

Avoid overly decorative religious imagery, stock-photo-looking church graphics, excessive gradients, cheesy fundraising visuals, or clutter.

The design should feel intentional and aspirational.

---

# ONE-PAGE STRUCTURE

Create a complete one-page landing page with the following sections.

## 1. HERO — "THE DREAM"

Create a visually powerful hero section.

Primary headline:

**K2K**

Supporting headline:

**A Place to Worship. A Place to Grow. A Place to Reach More.**

Alternative supporting message:

**Together, we can build a place where generations can encounter God.**

Show the fundraising goal prominently:

**₱10,000,000**

Include a short explanation:

**Our dream is to acquire a larger place of worship for Emmanuel's Living Gospel Church — a place where we can worship, disciple, serve, and reach more people with the Gospel.**

Primary CTA:

**JOIN THE MOVEMENT**

Secondary CTA:

**SEE OUR PROGRESS**

Include ELGC branding:

**Emmanuel's Living Gospel Church**

The hero should immediately communicate that this is a major long-term church vision.

---

# 2. THE STORY — "IT STARTED WITH 1,000"

Create a storytelling section explaining the history of K2K.

Use a large visual timeline or editorial layout.

Highlight:

**1,000 PEOPLE**

×

**₱1,000**

=

**₱1,000,000**

Then explain that K2K began around 15 years ago with a simple idea:

If 1,000 people each committed ₱1,000, together we could raise ₱1 million for a church property.

But the vision didn't stop there.

The world changed.
The church grew.
The needs grew.
And the dream grew.

Today:

**₱1,000,000 → ₱10,000,000**

Make this transition visually dramatic.

Possible headline:

**The goal grew. The vision grew with it.**

---

# 3. WHY WE NEED A NEW PLACE

Create an emotional section explaining why acquiring a larger property matters.

Do not make this sound like "we need a bigger building because our current building is small."

Instead communicate the ministry vision.

Use 4–6 visual cards:

### WORSHIP

A place where generations can gather and worship God together.

### DISCIPLESHIP

A place where people can grow deeper in their relationship with Jesus.

### COMMUNITY

A place where families, young people, and the wider community can belong.

### OUTREACH

A place where we can serve our community and share the Gospel.

### NEXT GENERATION

A place where children and young people can be equipped to follow Jesus.

### MISSION

A place that allows ELGC to continue fulfilling God's calling for the church.

Use strong photography or subtle visual treatments from the uploaded aesthetic.

---

# 4. THE BIG VISION

Create a large editorial-style section titled:

**IMAGINE WHAT GOD CAN DO WITH A PLACE LIKE THIS.**

Show an aspirational visualization / architectural concept / abstract representation of the future church property.

If actual architectural imagery is not available, create an elegant conceptual placeholder rather than inventing a realistic church building.

Overlay short statements such as:

**A place to worship.**

**A place to disciple.**

**A place to serve.**

**A place to send.**

**A place for generations.**

Make this section feel emotional and cinematic.

---

# 5. FUNDRAISING PROGRESS — THE MOST IMPORTANT INTERACTIVE ELEMENT

Create a visually striking fundraising progress section.

Headline:

**WE'RE BUILDING THIS TOGETHER.**

Show:

**₱10,000,000 GOAL**

and a large progress indicator.

Example placeholder:

**₱2,450,000 raised**

**24.5%**

Make the progress number extremely prominent.

Include:

**Every peso moves the vision forward.**

**I WANT TO BE PART OF K2K**

Also include smaller statistics:

**TOTAL RAISED**

**K2K PARTNERS**

**DAYS / YEARS OF THE JOURNEY**

Use placeholders where exact numbers are not yet available.

# MOTION / INTERACTION DIRECTION

Design the page with subtle interactions in mind.

Potential animations:

* ₱10M counter animates upward when entering viewport
* Fundraising progress bar animates when visible
* Timeline milestones reveal progressively on scroll
* Cards have subtle hover movement
* K2K logo has a subtle entrance animation
* Supporter wall slowly reveals additional entries
* Final CTA has a subtle visual emphasis

Keep animation tasteful and premium.

Avoid excessive animations.

---

# COPY / TONE

The tone should be:

**Faith-filled but not preachy.**

**Inspirational but not cheesy.**

**Urgent but not manipulative.**

**Community-driven rather than transactional.**

The visitor should leave thinking:

> "I want to be part of this."

Not:

> "They're asking me for money."

---

# VISUAL PRIORITIES

The most visually dominant elements should be:

1. **K2K**
2. **₱10,000,000**
3. **The vision for a new church home**
4. **Fundraising progress**
5. **Join the movement CTA**

Create strong contrast between large typography and whitespace.

Use the uploaded aesthetic reference as the source of truth for colors, typography, imagery, spacing, and overall visual personality.

Do not introduce a completely different visual style.

* Card styles

Design both desktop and mobile versions where appropriate.

The final result should look like a **real, launch-ready campaign website**, not a generic AI-generated landing page.

Most importantly:

**Make K2K feel like a movement the entire ELGC congregation can rally behind.**

# "WE CAN DO THIS TOGETHER."
